That row is ratified by issue #1 / DR-006, and the bound below cites it verbatim, not an invented final limit. Two discrete points, not a continuous 'dc iload 0 50m ...' sweep -- a continuous sweep was tried during this testbench's bring-up and did not track a single regulating branch across the whole range (a 51-point sweep at tt/27C/VIN=3.3V measured a 1.46V peak-to-peak vout excursion, over 80% of the 1.8V target, vs. ~4mV at the same corner's two endpoints via discrete .op solves); this is the same DC-solution-multiplicity family design/README.md's dated 2026-08-25 root-cause section documents for VIN sweeps (issue #60 mechanism 4, tracked by #71), now also observed load-current-side. Initial-condition contract (#171, converted by #172): each of the two points is a COLD-START, SETTLED transient ('tran 10u 200m uic' with the testbench's VEN stepping high at 101us, v(vout) averaged over the settled 199ms-200ms tail), NOT the bare unconstrained '.op' this bench used through record 20260925-111601-7701e7e. Every node starts at its natural cold value with the block disabled and powers up through its own soft-start ramp, so there is no operating-point solve in the deck at all -- the contract's 'uic' + EN-edge shape, the one PR #170 landed for sim/mc-output-accuracy. That matters because an unconstrained '.op' is not a physically realizable state of this block (#164 / PR #170: 12 of 200 mc-output-accuracy draws settled with FB above the only node feeding it, up to 1.3e15 V), and because the superseded record is the worst case of the same defect: 44 of its 45 corners carry a solver diagnostic (40 'singular matrix', 4 'out of range for ^') on the same three sky130_fd_pr__res_xhigh_po instances (xldo.ea_cz, xldo.amp_enn, xldo.n_fbb), which sim/bin/corner-run.py's #171 gate now forces to FAIL outright. Measured during #172 and the reason these are 'uic' transients rather than seeded 'op' cards: a seeded DC solve does not close the gap in ngspice -- '.ic' is honoured only for the operating-point solve preceding a non-'uic' transient and a bare 'op' ignores it, while '.nodeset', which a bare 'op' does honour, still printed 'singular matrix' plus 'Dynamic gmin stepping failed' at 3 of 5 corners probed. WHY 200ms AND NOT 1ms, this bench's own hard-won number: at the ratified row's 0mA endpoint the feedback divider is the only discharge path out of the 1uF C_OUT, so v(vout) at tt/27C/3.30V reads 1.85985V averaged over 0.8ms-1ms and only reaches its settled 1.80239V after ~100ms (1.83245V at 50ms, 1.80332V at 100ms, then flat to six digits through 200ms/300ms/400ms) -- a 1ms window reports a 57mV load regulation that is pure unsettled-transient artifact, 3x the ratified bound, while the 50mA endpoint settles inside 1ms at every corner probed. The 199ms-200ms window is measured, not assumed: at tt/125C/3.63V, ff/125C/3.63V and fs/125C/3.63V the 99ms-100ms and 199ms-200ms tail averages agree to <=0.1mV, and an independently seeded ('.ic', non-'uic') 400ms transient lands on the same tail at all three. load_reg_v is abs(vout_50ma - vout_0ma); load_reg_pct expresses the same delta as a percentage of the 1.8V ratified Output target, matching design/README.md's own load-regulation screening convention (which also uses discrete no-load/full-load points, not a sweep). Uses the DR-002 proposed nominal C_out/ESR (1uF/10mOhm), not a window sweep -- same convention as load-transient/dropout-vs-load/psrr-dc. Known risk, not a testbench defect: design/README.md's dated 2026-08-25 root-cause section (issue #60, mechanism 1; tracked by #69) documents a thermal-shutdown false-trip at the ff/sf process corners at 125C, independent of load current -- #69 re-sized that shutdown, and #172's cold-start re-run is this bench's first 45-corner record against the post-#69 DUT with a physically realizable start state, so its ff/sf 125C numbers are a fresh measurement rather than the pre-#69 false-trip expectation.
