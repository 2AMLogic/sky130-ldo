The row is DRAFT (issue #1 not yet ratified) and the stability window it quantifies over is DR-002's *proposed* one (C_eff 0.33-4.7 uF), also unratified, so the bounds below cite those DRAFT/proposed numbers directly rather than an invented final limit. Each of the four legs is an independent COLD enable: EN sits at 0 V until t = 100 us and ngspice computes each transient's own t = 0 operating point from the source's time-zero value, so every leg starts from a disabled, fully discharged block rather than from a settled solution. Two readings of the row are made explicit rather than left implicit. (1) "inside +-2% within a few ms of enable": this testbench reads "a few ms" as 3 ms after the enable edge and checks the output is inside the DRAFT Output row's 1.764-1.836 V window continuously from there to the end of the run -- 3 ms is this testbench's stated interpretation of an unquantified phrase in a DRAFT row, not a ratified number, and it is the one thing here that would need revisiting if #1 quantifies the phrase differently. (2) "monotonic ... controlled ramp": the numeric consequence of a non-monotonic or uncontrolled ramp is an excursion outside that same +-2% window, which vpk_* (peak over the whole post-enable run, bounded at the row's own overshoot <= +2%) and vmin_* (floor over the settled window) do bound; a direct sample-by-sample monotonicity predicate is NOT computed here, and the 10-90% ramp time is reported instead as the "controlled ramp" witness. That is a stated coverage limitation of this experiment, not a claim that monotonicity is proven.
