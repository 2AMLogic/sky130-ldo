- **Claim**: Substantiates spec/target-spec.md's ratified "Line regulation" row (< 5 mV/V over 2.97-3.63V, at 1mA and 50mA) against the LDO core-regulation-loop schematic landed by #14 (design/ldo_3v3in_1v8out.sch), using the row's own two named load points and the ratified range's own two endpoints. That row is ratified by issue #1 / DR-006, and the bound below cites it verbatim, not an invented final limit. Each corner runs four discrete operating points (VIN in {2.97V, 3.63V} x I_LOAD in {1mA, 50mA}, via the deck's own 'alter' on VIN, on EN's high level and on the load current source, mirroring sim/loop-gain's and sim/iq's multi-point convention), NOT a continuous 'dc vvin ...' sweep -- a continuous sweep over this exact range was tried during this testbench's bring-up and repeatedly hit gmin-stepping/singular-matrix non-convergence partway through (design/README.md's dated 2026-08-25 root-cause section, issue #60 mechanism 4, tracked by #71, already documents this DC-solution-multiplicity behavior for VIN sweeps at 50mA load), taking minutes per corner without completing; four independent solves at the range's own endpoints reproduce design/README.md's own screening line-regulation numbers (its 'DC operating grid' section also uses discrete VIN points, not a sweep). line_reg_<n>ma_mv_per_v = abs(1000*(vout_hi-vout_lo)/(3.63-2.97)), matching that screening convention. Initial-condition contract (#171, converted by #172): each of the four points is a COLD-START, SETTLED transient ('tran 10u 200m uic' with the testbench's VEN stepping high at 101us, v(vout) averaged over the settled 199ms-200ms tail), NOT the bare unconstrained '.op' this bench used through record 20260925-114251-1f54ca6. Every node starts at its natural cold value with the block disabled and powers up through its own soft-start ramp, so there is no operating-point solve in the deck at all -- the contract's 'uic' + EN-edge shape, the one PR #170 landed for sim/mc-output-accuracy. That matters because an unconstrained '.op' is not a physically realizable state of this block (#164 / PR #170: 12 of 200 mc-output-accuracy draws settled with FB above the only node feeding it, up to 1.3e15 V), and because the superseded record is the worst case of the same defect: all 45 of its corners carry a solver diagnostic (44 'singular matrix', 1 'Dynamic gmin stepping failed' plus 'out of range for ^') on the same three sky130_fd_pr__res_xhigh_po instances (xldo.ea_cz, xldo.amp_enn, xldo.n_fbb), which sim/bin/corner-run.py's #171 gate now forces to FAIL outright. Measured during #172 and the reason these are 'uic' transients rather than seeded 'op' cards: a seeded DC solve does not close the gap in ngspice -- '.ic' is honoured only for the operating-point solve preceding a non-'uic' transient and a bare 'op' ignores it, while '.nodeset', which a bare 'op' does honour, still printed 'singular matrix' plus 'Dynamic gmin stepping failed' at 3 of 5 corners probed. Cross-check that the start state is not steering the answer: an '.ic'-seeded non-'uic' settled transient reaches the same tail, agreeing to six digits on all four points at tt/27C/3.30V and to 31.2/43.9 mV/V at tt/125C/2.97V. EN convention (issue #196), which record 20260926-001833-228fbc7 -- the last full-matrix record taken under the old convention -- predates: EN's high level is the INSTANTANEOUS VIN at every measured point, 'alter'ed alongside VVIN from one per-point literal, NOT the corner runner's 'vsup' label as it was through record 20260926-001833-228fbc7. On this bench 'vsup' and VIN are genuinely different quantities -- VVIN is the bench's own source being swept 2.97-3.63V INSIDE each corner, while 'vsup' is fixed per corner -- so the old convention ran the VIN=3.63V point of every *_2.97v corner with EN 0.66V BELOW VIN. That is the EN-below-VIN geometry #187 root-caused on sim/dropout-vs-load (EN gates five PMOS shutdown clamps whose SOURCES ARE VIN, so their gate drive is VIN-EN; at 0.66V down two of them out-drive the error amplifier's tail current, EA_OUT is pulled to VIN and M_PASS turns off), it is a static headroom limit rather than a start-up branch, and it leaves NO solver diagnostic -- which is why #171's gate did not catch it. It accounted for every one of the 5 failing corners of the superseded record, which were exactly the *_125c_2.97v column. This convention is chosen over 'EN = 3.63V at every corner' (which would put EN ABOVE VIN at the low point, a different off-contract state over-driving the EN-gated NMOS switches) and over collapsing the supply axis for this row (a manifest change to the ratified corner matrix's meaning, which CLAUDE.md routes through a decision record), because it is the only one that honours design/README.md's documented 'Enable/shutdown' interface -- 'active-high, full-rail (0V / VIN)' -- at 2.97V and 3.63V alike. No design or spec line moves: #196 is a bench-convention fix. Consequence to read any record from this deck with: 'vsup' is now a pure corner LABEL on this bench and is referenced by nothing in its stimulus, so a corner's 2.97V/3.30V/3.63V variants are the same experiment and their columns are expected to be IDENTICAL -- the supply axis is retained as a harness reproducibility check rather than collapsed. Uses the DR-002 proposed nominal C_out/ESR (1uF/10mOhm), not a window sweep -- same convention as load-transient/dropout-vs-load/psrr-dc. Known risk, not a testbench defect: the same design/README.md section (mechanism 1) documents a thermal-shutdown false-trip at the ff/sf process corners at 125C, independent of load current -- tracked by #69, which re-sized that shutdown. #172's cold-start re-run is this bench's first 45-corner record against the post-#69 DUT with a physically realizable start state, so its ff/sf 125C numbers are a fresh measurement rather than the pre-#69 false-trip expectation.
