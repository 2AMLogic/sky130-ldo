# Issue #349 -- load-adaptive compensation prototype (screening diagnostic)

Status: measured screening, local single-corner probes only. **No spec row, DR status or production schematic
is changed.** Nothing here qualifies the Stability window. All numbers are in `tables.md` (generated by
`compare.py` from `runs/*/result.json`); this file interprets them.

Hypothesis under test (from the #324 mitigation-screen README, "Load-adaptive compensation hypothesis"): split
R_CZ into R_a + (R_b || triode PMOS), drive the PMOS gate from the existing load-sense path, so R_CZ is about
today's value at light load and about half of it at heavy load.

## 1. What was built (netlist-level only; `design/` untouched)

Edits are applied to the xschem-generated testbench netlist by `adaptive_probe.py` (raw + edited netlists saved
and hashed per run):

- **Split resistor**: `XR_CZA` (EA_CZ -> RCZ_MID, W 0.42 um, L = frac x 52 um) + `XR_CZB` (RCZ_MID -> EA_OUT,
  L = (1-frac) x 52 um), series total length unchanged. `XM_CZSW` = `pfet_g5v0d10v5` L 0.5 W 2 um (bulk VIN)
  across R_b, gate = `ADAPT_G`. Switch off (gate high): R_CZ = R_a + R_b ~ today's 300.6k. Switch on:
  R_CZ ~ R_a.
- **Control circuit, derived from the measured sense path (section 2)**: `XM_CLN3` (`nfet_g5v0d10v5` L 1, W = W3,
  gate = CL_SNS, source = AMP_ENN, drain = ADAPT_G) mirrors the sense diode `XM_CLN1`'s Vgs (a W3/20 copy of
  the M_SENSE replica current); `XM_ADPU` (`pfet_g5v0d10v5` L 1 W 1.25, gate = BIASP, i.e. the same 1/8-bias
  unit geometry as the existing `XM_TSPS`) pulls ADAPT_G to VIN. ADAPT_G therefore falls from VIN to ~0 once
  the mirrored sense current exceeds the pull-up current -- polarity matches the PMOS switch (on at heavy load).
  A bare CL_SNS cannot drive the switch: it spans only 0.35 -> 0.88 V (tt) and is a diode-connected node whose
  loading would move the current-limit threshold; the gate-only tap adds no DC current to it.

Two realizations were carried to paired probes (the issue's "at most two"), both with C_COMP at the current
150 pF:

| id | R_a / R_b of today's total | heavy-load R_CZ | tap W3 | measured transition (gate crosses ~VIN/2) |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| R1 | 0.5 / 0.5 | 0.5x | 6 um | ~3 mA at tt/27C |
| R2 | 0.4 / 0.6 | 0.4x | 4 um | ~6 mA at tt/27C |

Exploration actually run to choose them (all tt/27C/3.30 V, kept in `runs/`): sense sweeps with W3 = 2, 4, 6,
10, 20 (`sense_tap*_tt`; transition ~18, 6, 3.3, 2, 0.9 mA); loop-gain screens `lg_s50_t6_c1_tt`,
`lg_s50_t6_c1_v2_tt`, `lg_s40_t4_c1_tt`, `lg_s60_t6_c1_tt`, `lg_s50_t6_c2_tt` (the last is C_COMP 2x, 0.5/0.5,
W3 6). C_COMP 2x was dropped: it lowered 4.7 uF / 0 mA PM (53.1 -> 40.7 deg, newly failing) and 0.33 uF / 0 mA
(19.7 -> 17.3). The 0.6/0.4 split left 0.33 uF / 50 mA at 62 deg (tt), the weakest heavy-load repair. No
Cartesian grid was run.

## 2. Control-range and sense-loading evidence (`sense_probe.py`, tables.md "Existing sense path")

- M_SENSE replica current rises monotonically with load: 0.001 uA (0 mA) -> 0.63 uA (1 mA) -> 12.76 uA
  (50 mA) at tt; CL_SNS 0.353 -> 0.712 -> 0.884 V. The sign is usable (more load = more current), the range is
  roughly logarithmic and small (60-100 mV/decade) -- hence the mirror-plus-pull-up comparator rather than a
  direct drive.
- ADAPT_G swings rail-to-rail: R1 W3 = 6 gives 3.30 V at 0 mA, 3.26 V at 1 mA, 0.25 V at 5 mA, 0.02 V at
  50 mA (tt).
- **Loading of the existing current-limit nodes is negligible**: tap-vs-baseline change in CL_SNS is within
  +/-0.5 mV and in CL_CMP within +/-0.01 mV at every sampled load and all three conditions. The only load-side
  cost is the pull-up current once the mirror conducts.
- **Control threshold moves with PVT**: R1 mid-point ~3.3 mA (tt), ~5 mA (ss/-40C: 0.997 V at 5 mA), ~3 mA
  (ff/125C: 1.95 V at 3 mA). R2's mid-point at ss/-40C is ABOVE 10 mA (0.525 V at 10 mA, switch still barely
  conducting) -- outside the issue's 1-10 mA window. The mirror runs in weak inversion against a pull-up
  derived from the bias mirror, so the threshold tracks process/temperature.
- **Switch strength is limited by the node it sits on**: the switch source is EA_OUT, which sits at 1.47 V at
  ss/-40C/2.97 V and 50 mA (1.94 V tt, 2.46 V ff). With the gate at ~0, gate overdrive at ss is only a few
  hundred mV, so the "on" resistance is large: measured R_eff/R_a at 50 mA = 1.04 (ff), 1.06 (tt), 1.41 (ss)
  for R1 instead of the ideal 1.0. A diagnostic probe with the switch widened 4x (`lg_diag_r1_sww8_ss`) only
  reached 1.22 (PM 60.4 deg, 0.33 uF/50 mA), so this is a headroom limit, not just a sizing choice. This
  probe is a diagnostic, not a third realization.

75k); R2 150.3k at <= 5 mA, 64k at >= 10 mA (target 60k).

- **Repaired**: the 0.33 uF / 50 mA failures at all three sampled conditions. R1 PM: 74.2 (tt) / 52.7 (ss) /

- **Not regressed** (the reason to be adaptive): 1 mA legs are unchanged to within 0.1 deg for both C_out

- **Unresolved, retained**: the 0 mA failures are untouched by construction (the switch is off at 0 mA; every 0 mA

- **Invalid, kept visible, never counted as pass**: baseline leg 11 (4.7u/500m/1mA) has no -180 deg crossing at any
  condition (GM missing); under R1 and R2 leg 10 (4.7u/500m/50mA) joins it at all three conditions (its PM is recorded
  in result.json -- R1: 96.9 / 103.7 / 93.7 deg at tt / ss / ff -- but GM is undefined, so it is not graded). The classifier treats a missing GM crossing as invalid, as the #324

## 4. Iq (unchanged accounting; adaptive circuit counted as internal consumption; nothing subtracted)

| condition | no-load Iq base -> R1 / R2 (uA) | 50 mA Iq base -> R1 / R2 (uA) | 50 mA < 30 uA? |
|---|---|---|---|
| tt/27C/3.30V | 13.871 -> 13.872 / 13.871 (+0.001) | 27.610 -> 28.240 (+0.630) | all yes |
| ss/-40C/2.97V | 11.822 -> 11.822 (+0.000) | 23.930 -> 24.510 (+0.580) | all yes |
| ff/125C/3.63V | 15.377 -> 15.378 (+0.001) | 30.680 -> 31.330 (+0.650) | **no -- baseline already fails (30.68)**; R1/R2 31.33 |

Incremental no-load cost is ~1 nA (inside the <= 0.5 uA exploration budget; the pull-up has no path to ground
until the mirror conducts). The incremental FULL-load cost is 0.58-0.65 uA (the pull-up current flows through
XM_CLN3 for the whole heavy-load range), and it exceeds the 0.5 uA figure, which the issue defined for no-load;
it enlarges the baseline ff/125C 50 mA Iq failure from 30.68 to 31.33 uA. The baseline over-budget corner is
independent of the adaptive circuit and stays open (DR-009 / #213); the 0.5 uA budget is not a substitute for the
absolute < 30 uA spec gate. Cross-check: `iq_*` (the sim/iq cold-start transient, manifest analyses verbatim)
agrees with the loop-gain `op` accounting to <= 0.01 uA.

## 5. Current limit, and the 1 <-> 50 mA transient (not full PVT qualification)

- **Overload (sim/current-limit, manifest analyses verbatim, paired at tt/ss/ff)**: ilim_short, ilim_knee,
  knee droop, cl_margin_50ma and sustained short current are unchanged to <= 0.01 percent for R1 and R2 against
  the same-session baseline at all three conditions (e.g. tt ilim_short 153.31 mA in all three; ff ilim_knee
  105.99 -> 106.00). Short-circuit ripple differs (tt 0.040 -> 0.012 / 0.006 percent) and is not worse. All 9
  current-limit runs valid, no solver diagnostic.
- **Load step (sim/load-transient, #313 six-leg window grid, 1 mA <-> 50 mA steps through the adaptation
  region, paired at tt/ss/ff)**: compensation switching is stable (all runs valid, settle error unchanged to
  <= 1e-4 percent) but is not clean:
  - rise undershoot grows: 0.33 uF / 10 mOhm tt 0.1916 -> 0.2045 (R1) / 0.2107 (R2) V; 4.7 uF / 10 mOhm tt
    0.0845 -> 0.1134 / 0.1227 V (+34 / +45 percent); ff 0.0988 -> 0.1321 / 0.1424 V.
  - fall overshoot grows and newly crosses the ratified 150 mV bound: tt 0.33 uF / ESR 0: 0.1421 -> 0.1501 (R1)
    / 0.1508 (R2); R2 also ESR 10 mOhm 0.1418 -> 0.1505; ff 0.33 uF / 500 mOhm 0.1429 -> 0.1586 / 0.1617.
  - 1 -> 50 mA recovery is faster (tt 96.7 -> 77.6 / 72.2 us), 50 -> 1 mA recovery is faster at 0.33 uF
    (71.9 -> 62.5 us) but slower at 4.7 uF (606 -> 714 / 720 us, +18 percent).
  - The baseline already fails several load-transient manifest bounds (0.33 uF undershoot, recovery rows); the
    candidates do not fix them and add the new overshoot_fall failures above. Those rows are owned by #345 /
    #313; this probe only compares like with like.

## 6. Recommendation: REVISE (do not advance either realization as-is; production adoption is a separate decision)

The hypothesis is supported in its narrow form and refuted as a complete answer:

- **Supported**: the existing sense path contains a usable, low-loading load-proportional control signal; a
  mirror-plus-pull-up tap derived from it drives a PDK PMOS across half of R_CZ; at three sampled PVT conditions
  it repairs 0.33 uF / 50 mA stability without the 4.7 uF / 1 mA regression of a fixed R_CZ cut, with ~1 nA no-load
  cost and no measurable current-limit change.
- **Reasons to revise rather than advance**: (a) the switching threshold moves with PVT enough to leave the
  1-10 mA window (R2 at ss/-40C) -- the control must be made PVT-tracking; (b) the PMOS switch is
  headroom-limited at ss/-40C (EA_OUT 1.47 V), leaving R_eff 1.4x R_a and the thinnest repair margin (52.7 deg) --
  a switch topology that does not hang on EA_OUT (e.g. an NMOS/transmission arrangement or a different
  insertion point) needs its own evaluation; (c) the 1 <-> 50 mA transient gets measurably worse, with new
  overshoot_fall bound crossings, so the transient interaction is a design input, not a footnote; (d) it adds
  0.6 uA at full load to a baseline that already fails the Iq row at ff/125C.
- **Not addressed, retained as open**: the 0 mA stability failures (0.33 uF at all ESR points at tt/ss; 4.7 uF at
  ss) and the baseline full-load Iq failure at ff/125C (30.68 uA). This lever neither fixes nor claims to fix
  them; they need a separate light-load lever (as the #324 README already noted).
- **If revised and re-advanced, the qualification handoff is** (nothing here certifies any of it): carry R1
  (R_a/R_b = 0.5/0.5 of L = 52 um, W 0.42, XM_CZSW W 2 L 0.5, XM_CLN3 W 6 L 1, XM_ADPU W 1.25 L 1, C_COMP
  150 pF) as the parameter baseline; add a PVT-tracking control and a headroom-robust switch; then run the FULL
  45-point matrix ({tt,ss,ff,sf,fs} x {-40,27,125 C} x {2.97,3.30,3.63 V}) for baseline AND candidate on
  loop-gain (26 legs incl. the transition-region legs and ESR boundary), iq, current-limit and the
  load-transient window grid, through `klt sim` on the batch backend once compatible batch execution is verified
  for those experiments (gating: #327's batch runner/client state is a prerequisite, not merely its closure; the
  window grid additionally follows #345). Add intermediate steps (e.g. 2 <-> 10 mA, 3 <-> 7 mA) for a
  hysteresis/chatter check, which was not run here.
