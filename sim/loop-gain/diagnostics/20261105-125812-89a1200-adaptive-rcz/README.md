# Light-load adaptive R_CZ — bounded physical screen (issue #370, stage 2)

Append-only diagnostic. Production schematic, spec and decision records are **unchanged**; the candidate exists
only as a text edit of the netlist (`sim/bin/rcz-adaptive-370.py`, `edit_candidate`). Stage 1 (fleet
confirmation of #365's AC pairs) is `../20261105-080222-a45f37b-fleet-confirm-365/`.

## Recommendation: **REVISE** candidate A; **REJECT** candidate B

Machine verdicts: `verdict.json`; every number below is in `tables.md` (generated by
`sim/bin/rcz-adaptive-370-report.py`). This is a screen at 1 supply (3.30 V), not a qualification claim.

## Realization (one topology, two parameter choices)

R_CZ is split into the stock 52 um xhigh_po segment plus a 780 um segment (total 832 um = #365's x16), and a
pfet switch `XM_RZSW` shorts the extra segment when the load is above a threshold. The control reuses the
**existing** current-limit sense mirror: `XM_LLN` (nfet L=1 W=20, 1:1 with `M_CLN1`) pulls the switch gate `GSW`
down against a small pfet reference current `XM_LLP` (gate BIASP); `XM_LLEN` forces `GSW` high when EN is low.
No new sense FET, so no extra sense current at heavy load. Parameter choices: reference pfet L = 11 um (**A**) or
2.2 um (**B**).

## Findings

1. **The sensing mechanism works and is nearly free.** The switch toggles sharply (tt/27: A between 10 and 30 uA,
   B between 100 and 300 uA). Effective R_CZ 301 kohm vs 4811 kohm (x16) is confirmed from instrumentation
   resistors. Iq delta vs baseline over 9 PVT corners: A <= +0.032 uA, B <= +0.140 uA (at 0 load: +0.002 uA).
   Absolute worst Iq at 50 mA: baseline 29.88, A 29.90, **B 30.02 uA (over the 30 uA row, which the baseline
   leaves 0.12 uA under)**.
2. **Zero load and <=10 uA are repaired** (tt/27): 0.33 uF PM 19.7 -> 65.0, 4.7 uF 53.1 -> 60.1, all three ESR
   boundaries; ss/ff x -40/125 C at 0 mA also pass for both candidates (baseline fails 0.33 uF at ss/-40 and ff/-40 and 4.7 uF at
   both -40 C corners). 1 mA and 50 mA are within ~1.2 deg of baseline (the 0.33 uF / 50 mA failure
   is the baseline's own, unchanged).
3. **A load-keyed threshold cannot close the 30-300 uA band.** The baseline itself fails PM >= 45 at 0.33 uF /
   30 uA and at 4.7 uF / 30, 100, 300 uA (and 10 uA). Fixed x16 repairs 4.7 uF there (75.8 / 69.8 / 50.8 deg) but
   breaks 0.33 uF at 100 and 300 uA (25.7 / 15.5). The two capacitance endpoints want opposite R_CZ in
   100-300 uA, so no single threshold on load current serves both. Candidate A (threshold ~20 uA) leaves
   30-300 uA at baseline behavior; candidate B (threshold ~150 uA) repairs 4.7 uF at 100 uA only where it is
   on the large-R_CZ side and **regresses 0.33 uF / 100 uA** (62.5 -> 25.8 at tt, and at ss/-40 and ss/125), so B is
   rejected.
4. **Threshold spread over PVT is large** (A: switch on by 10 uA at ff, not until 100 uA at ss/-40), see the
   switch-state table in `tables.md`.
5. **Transients.** Stage-1 deferred pair now confirmed on the fleet: x16 vs baseline 1<->50 mA improves recovery
   (4.7 uF falling edge 605 -> 104 us at tt/27; rise 98 -> 53 us) with lower peaks, matching #365's provisional
   numbers. Candidates leave the rising 1 -> 50 mA edge and the 0 <-> 1 mA rise at baseline; the **falling
   50 -> 1 mA edge at 0.33 uF recovers more slowly than baseline** (72 -> 126 us for A, 134 us for B at tt/27;
   both already exceed the 20 us bound) while the 4.7 uF falling edge recovers faster (605 -> 265 us). The
   0.33 uF 1 mA -> 0 falling case is "NOT RECOVERED" for baseline and A: with no load the overshoot (22-36 mV, > the
   18 mV band) discharges only through the feedback divider (tau ~ C * 2.6 Mohm), so this case measures discharge
   limitation, not loop stability; B's lower overshoot (17-31 mV) is not a stability claim either. One baseline
   anomaly is recorded unaltered: ff/-40 f50->1 mA peak 223 mV, not recovered (candidates 125 mV there).

## NOT run — cannot count as PASS

- **Current-limit interaction** (`XM_LLN` loads the `CL_SNS` mirror node; the current-limit threshold and overload
  response were not measured). Required before any advance.
- Loop gain at loads *between* the sampled points (10/30/100/300 uA): the partially-on transition (e.g. A at
  tt/125, 10 uA, R_CZ 1129 kohm) was seen only at the DC operating point.
- Loop gain at ESR 0 / 500 mOhm for loads > 0 and at supplies other than 3.30 V; PVT loop gain was run only at
  ss/ff x -40/125 C for 0, 100 uA, 1 and 50 mA (no tt/27 baseline pairing issue: tt/27 is in the main table).
- Transients at supply != 3.30 V, ESR != 10 mOhm and 0 <-> 100 uA steps; the full 45-PVT x 18-leg qualification
  (separate handoff, #231). Monte Carlo / mismatch of the reference pfet and mirror.
- Rebaseline if #357 changes the DUT.

## Remaining qualification matrix if a revised candidate advances

A revised candidate needs a discriminator that is not a pure load threshold across the 30-300 uA band
(for example a second condition or a different compensation element), then: 45-PVT x 18-leg loop gain with the
intermediate loads above, 1<->50 mA and 0<->1 mA transients at each PVT point, Iq at 0/50 mA at every corner, and
current-limit/overload with the added mirror load.
