- **Experiment**: `dropout-vs-load` — Dropout voltage at 50mA load (Vin sweep toward Vout, DR-003/gf180 convention)
- **Claim**: Substantiates spec/target-spec.md's ratified "Dropout @ 50 mA" row (< 300mV, stretch goal < 200mV) against the LDO core-regulation-loop schematic landed by #14 (design/ldo_3v3in_1v8out.sch), using the ratified row's own cited method ("gf180's ss/125C/Vin~=Vout+dropout convention"): fix I_LOAD at 50mA (the row's own test point) and sweep Vin down toward Vout to find the Vin-Vout margin at which regulation is lost, rather than sweeping load current at a single ample Vin (that measures load regulation, a different spec row). That row is ratified by issue #1 / DR-006, and the bound below cites it verbatim, not an invented final limit. Methodology fixed by issue #71 (superseding #18's original method): the deck sweeps VIN downward, from a comfortably-regulating value toward a value well below the 1.8V target, at a finer (20mV) resolution than #18's original 50mV; dropout_v is the Vin-Vout margin at the VIN where VOUT first falls (`.meas dc ... fall=1`) through 98% of the 1.8V target as VIN decreases -- the classic "regulation just lost" dropout definition -- not a fixed low-VIN endpoint (#18's method reported the pass device's residual V_sd well past dropout, overstating the gap; see #71's PR for the derivation and design/README.md's campaign section for the closed-loop screening that motivated the fix). vout_at_max_vin_v is a light-headroom regulation sanity check at the sweep's top (first-swept) end. INITIAL-CONDITION CONTRACT (#171, converted by #178): the analysis is no longer a `dc VVIN 3.63 1.5 -0.02` sweep. It is ONE cold-start, settled transient ('tran 10u 14m uic') in which VIN is a PWL ramp -- 0V to the corner's own 'vsup' top by 100us, EN stepping high at 100us to that same 'vsup' (issue #187), the ideal 50mA sink ramped on over 2.5-2.6ms once the loop is up (sim/ic-screen-125c-c's own cold-start convention, verbatim), settled by 5.5ms, then VIN walked DOWN 'vsup'->1.5V over 6ms-14ms (183.8/225.0/266.3 V/s at 2.97/3.30/3.63V) as a quasi-static line droop -- so there is no operating-point solve in the deck at all: the contract's 'uic' + EN-edge shape, the one PR #170 landed for mc-output-accuracy and #172 for line-/load-regulation. WHY a shape change rather than a seed: ngspice honours neither accepted seed shape for a `dc` analysis. Measured during #178 on this bench (tt/125C/3.30V, the eleven-node `.ic` card copied verbatim from sim/ic-screen-125c-b): the seeded `dc` sweep flails exactly as the unseeded one does -- 'singular matrix' x6, 'Dynamic gmin stepping failed' x16, 'out of range for ^' x13, with iterates at 1e155-1e188 V on the sky130_fd_pr__res_xhigh_po body expressions -- because `.ic` is applied to the operating-point solve preceding a non-'uic' transient, not to a `dc` sweep's solves; and #172 measured the other half, that `.nodeset` (which a bare solve does honour) still printed 'singular matrix' plus 'Dynamic gmin stepping failed' at 3 of 5 corners probed. A seed could in any case only fix the sweep's FIRST point, and the superseded record 20260923-123440-d71f4b3 carries thousands of diagnostics per corner log (45 of 45 corners diagnosed), i.e. the continuation loses the branch repeatedly all the way down the sweep -- which sim/bin/corner-run.py's #171 gate now forces to FAIL outright. QUASI-STATIC, MEASURED not assumed -- a three-point ramp-rate series at tt/27C/3.30V, all three with zero solver diagnostics: 8ms ramp (266.3 V/s) reads dropout_v = 0.397657V, 20ms ramp (106.5 V/s) reads 0.397295V, 60ms ramp (35.5 V/s) reads 0.398492V. The spread over a 7.5x rate range is 1.2mV with no monotone trend -- the answer is rate-INDEPENDENT in this window, not merely 'slow enough' -- and the superseded `dc` record, the zero-rate limit of the same experiment, reads 0.399743V at that corner, 2.1mV above the 8ms ramp (the `dc` sweep's own 20mV grid interpolation). The whole 2.4mV span sits against a 300mV ratified bound; the matrix runs the 8ms ramp because it is inside the rate-independent window and costs 22s per corner where the 60ms ramp cost 471s. SUPPLY AXIS, corrected by issue #187: 'vsup' now sets the DUT's actual VIN -- the ramp starts at 'vsup' and EN's high level is that same 'vsup' -- rather than EN's rail alone. Through record 20260926-043132-eba96ae (#178) VIN was pinned at 3.63V for every corner, inherited unchanged from the `dc` sweep's own first point, while 'vsup' drove EN only; that put EN 0.66V BELOW VIN at the '*_2.97v' corners, which is off the DUT's documented enable interface (design/README.md "Enable/shutdown": EN is active-high, full-rail 0V/VIN) and is what stopped the block coming up at fs_125c_2.97v (vout_at_max_vin_v = -7.4521V there, with no solver diagnostic). #187 measured the mechanism -- the M_ENP/M_ENP2 shutdown clamps conduct 1.377uA/1.639uA at EN = VIN-0.66V against 39pA/62pA at EN = VIN, while the amplifier's own tail current falls from 3.476uA to 1.003uA because M_ENP2's injection lifts BIASP 90.6mV -- and replaced the convention with the one every other bench in sim/ already uses (VIN = 'vsup', EN = 'vsup'). dropout_v is unaffected within ~1mV (measured: fs/125C/2.97V 0.432662V vs its 3.30V/3.63V siblings' 0.432204V/0.432251V), because the crossing sits near 2.2V, far below every supply column's starting point, and all three per-supply ramp rates are inside the rate-independent window measured above.
