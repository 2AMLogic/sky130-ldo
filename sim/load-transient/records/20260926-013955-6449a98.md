Both the spec row and the C_out/ESR window (spec/decision-records/DR-002) are ratified by issue #1 / DR-006, and the bounds below cite those numbers verbatim, not an invented final limit. This testbench's undershoot/overshoot measurements are computed as the deviation from the pre-step (light-load) steady-state value, not an absolute vout bound. Issue #180 brought this bench into conformance with sim/README.md's "Initial-condition contract (issue #171)": the transient is released from an eleven-node `.ic`-constrained operating point (#164's variant-B shape, PR #170 -- see testbench/tb_load_transient.sch's header and this manifest's deck.analyses comments) instead of from ngspice's own unconstrained loop-closed `.op`, which 23 of the 45 corner logs in the superseded 20260825-081255-4cb27f8 record show hitting a singular-matrix / gmin-stepping diagnostic. The stimulus, the step timestamps, the C_out/ESR point and every measurement bound are unchanged by that conversion; the pre-step reference moved from v(vout)[0] to a 0.800-0.999ms average and the peak searches were windowed to the stepped region, because under an `.ic` seed t=0 is the seed itself rather than the corner's own settled 1mA point. The row has TWO independent clauses and, until #119, this manifest measured only the first: undershoot_v/overshoot_v cover "peak excursion <=150mV", and recovery_rise_us/recovery_fall_us (added in #119) cover "recover to +-1% in <=20us" on the rising and falling load edges respectively. Both clause bounds are quoted verbatim from the same ratified row. Issue #119 also moved C_out from the superseded record's 1uF (DR-002's recommended nominal) to 4.7uF (DR-002's ratified window's own ceiling, still 0-500mOhm ESR -- not a value outside the ratified window) to address the peak-excursion clause, which #119 root-caused as charge-limited (Q=C*dV against the loop's finite large-signal response time, not phase-margin-limited -- see design/README.md's #115 anti-correlation finding). C_out/ESR remain a single representative point inside the ratified window, not a sweep of the window -- walking the window inside one deck the way sim/loop-gain does is still follow-on scope (e.g. #19). See spec/decision-records/DR-002's append and design/README.md's "#119" section for the full per-corner evidence, including the recovery-time clause's own, opposite-direction relationship to C_out (this record's own #119 writeup) that this C_out choice does not close. Known-immature-design caveat (design/README.md "Known gaps"): C_COMP/C_CL remain unsized placeholders and the error amplifier still has a light-load/high-VIN output-swing ceiling (design/README.md's "Known open item") even though issue #22 already added the current-limit and soft-start protection circuitry, so a FAIL here -- especially at the high-VIN corner -- is an honest, expected finding at this design stage (per CLAUDE.md's "Verification is the product"), not a harness defect.
