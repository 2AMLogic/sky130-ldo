"At Vin_max" is covered by the corner runner's own supply axis (the 3.63 V column), not by a hard-coded VIN. The short is applied as an event at t=0.5ms and HELD to t=2.5ms -- 2 ms of continuous Vout = 0, a defined duration rather than a single sampled instant, since "survives" is a claim about holding the fault. Known-caveat, stated rather than hidden (design/README.md, "Known accuracy caveat"): M_SENSE is a 0.42um replica of a 2500um pass device whose drain does not sit at VOUT, so the 1:5952 ratio is neither Vds-matched nor trimmed and carries substantial random Vth mismatch -- this corner-matrix experiment measures the systematic PVT window only; the mismatch axis would need a Monte Carlo experiment (sim/bin/mc-run.py), which is not in this issue's scope.
