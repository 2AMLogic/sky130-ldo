One decision per record. Copy this file to `DR-NNN-<slug>.md` and fill in
every section below. Do not leave a section empty — write "N/A" with a reason
if a section genuinely does not apply.

Records are **append-only**: a record that turns out to be wrong is superseded
by a new record, never quietly edited to match a later outcome. A record does
not ratify itself — say plainly what ratifies it.

## Context

What prompted this decision? What constraint, open question, or conflict
needs resolving? Cite the spec row(s) or issue(s) this touches, and the
device/PDK facts the argument rests on (with the pinned PDK version, if the
argument depends on model data).

## Decision

State the decision plainly, in one or two sentences. If this record is
`proposed` rather than `ratified`, say so explicitly and name what
ratifies it (e.g. an operator-only issue).

## Alternatives considered

Every alternative that was seriously weighed, and why it was not chosen (or,
if deferred rather than rejected, what would need to be true to revisit it).
State the rejected option's case fairly enough that someone who preferred it
would recognise their own argument.

## Consequences

What this decision fixes, unblocks, or constrains downstream — including the
costs, not just the benefits. Name the spec rows or design choices that must
change as a result, and the problems the decision **hands to design
unresolved** rather than settling.

## Status notes

Any conditions attached to the current status (e.g. "stays `proposed` until
issue #N closes"), and where to look for the ratifying action.
